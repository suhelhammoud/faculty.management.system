# Asset Maintenance System

## Purpose

Manages IT equipment maintenance requests and tracks repair progress.

## Users and workflows

Requesters and people handling repairs are implied; their identities, workflow steps, and status model are open.

## Permissions and dependencies

No module-specific permissions or dependencies are defined. Shared sign-in and access are described in [identity and access](../../identity-and-access.md). Relationship to Inventory is undecided.

## Open decisions

- Who can submit, assign, update, and close a request?
- What request details, repair states, and reporting are needed?
- How, if at all, does maintenance relate to equipment tracked in Inventory?
